Showing the Mac as a display on Windows gets options, stats, and dragging between screens.

## New
- **Choose where and how.** The first time you click **Show as display** (and any time from **Options…**), pick:
  - **Where:** full screen on one of your monitors, or in a window.
  - **Size:** match the screen or window, or a fixed 4K, 1440p or 1080p.
  - **Frame rate:** up to 144 fps. Rates the Mac can't keep up with at that size are greyed out: 60 at 4K, 120 at 1440p, 144 at 1080p.
- **Resizes to fit.** With "match", the Mac's display changes size half a second after you finish resizing the window or toggling full screen (F11).
- **Arranged like your desk.** The Mac places its extra display on the side of the MacBook where you're showing it. Full screen on the monitor above the laptop puts it above the MacBook's screen.
- **Drag windows between the Mac's screens.**
  - Drag a Mac window off the edge of the display window and it carries on onto the MacBook's screen.
  - Move onto the extra display from the MacBook's screen and the pointer comes out in the display window.
  - With the display full screen, push past the edge towards the MacBook to cross over.
- **Stats overlay.** Resolution, frame rate, bitrate, and how far the picture trails the Mac, split into the Mac, the network, decoding and display. Toggle it with F10 or in Settings.
- **Lower-latency presentation on Windows.** New frames replace ones waiting to be shown, with no tearing, on any DirectX 12 GPU.

## Updating
**Update both machines**: this version won't connect to earlier ones.

On the Mac, macOS treats each unsigned build as a new app, so permissions granted to an earlier version don't carry over, even though Legato still shows as allowed. Quit Legato, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Open Legato again and allow each prompt.
