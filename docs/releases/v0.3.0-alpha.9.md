Adaptive quality: a sharp 4K picture while you read and type, and 1080p speed while you drag and scroll.

## New
- **Adaptive stream quality, now the default.** When the Mac's screen is still or changing a little (typing, a cursor blinking), the picture is sent at full resolution. When a lot of it keeps moving (dragging a window, scrolling), it's sent at 1920×1080 straight away, at up to 144 fps. About 150 ms after things stop, the full-resolution picture comes back. A single big change, like switching tabs, stays sharp rather than flashing soft. Choose it, and the size while moving (1920×1080 or 2560×1440), in **Options…** next to your Mac. Sharpest, Balanced and Fastest are still there.
- The stats overlay (F10) says **while moving** when it's showing the smaller picture.

## Fixed
- A keystroke or other small change on a 4K display that's streaming at high frame rates is now sent at once, instead of waiting a frame or two behind the last one.
- When the network backed up just as the screen went still, the viewer could be left showing a slightly old picture until something changed. The latest picture is now sent once the backlog clears.

## Updating
**Update both machines:** this version won't connect to earlier ones.

On the Mac, quit Legato, install the new version, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Then open Legato and allow the prompts.
