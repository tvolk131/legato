The PC and the Mac share one pointer: whichever mouse you pick up carries on from where the pointer is.

## Fixed
- **No more jump when you switch mice.** Before, if you drove the Mac from the PC, then used the Mac's trackpad, then moved the PC's mouse again, the pointer jumped back to where the PC last left it (often the seam between the two machines). Now the PC keeps track of where the trackpad took the pointer and carries on from there. This works the other way round too, when the Mac is driving the PC.
- **The pointer stays on the Mac when the Mac's own trackpad or keyboard is used.** Until now, the PC's cursor came back on the PC's screen at the seam. Now the pointer stays where it is, on the Mac. The PC's cursor stays hidden until the pointer comes back across or you move the PC's mouse.
- **With the Mac's extra display shown on the PC**, moving the Mac's pointer off that display onto the MacBook hides the PC's cursor, instead of leaving it at the picture's edge. The PC's mouse then carries on from the MacBook.

## Changed
- **Keys and clicks follow the pointer, like the mouse.** While the pointer is on the PC, typing or clicking on the MacBook takes it back and goes to the PC, just as moving the trackpad does, and the same goes the other way round. To type on the Mac, bring the pointer back across.

## Still to come
- The Mac's trackpad can't yet move the pointer from its extra display onto the PC's other monitors.

## Updating
**Both machines need this version.** It won't connect to 0.3.0-alpha.16 or earlier.

On the Mac, quit Legato, install the new version, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Then open Legato and allow the prompts.
