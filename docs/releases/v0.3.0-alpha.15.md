The Mac's trackpad can drive the PC.

## Fixed
- **Pushing the Mac's pointer onto the PC with the MacBook's trackpad works.** When the Mac takes over another machine, it parks its hidden cursor in the middle of its screen. macOS then reports that jump as part of the next movement, so crossing up onto the PC immediately read as a big push back down to the Mac, and the jump back read as a push up again. The pointer bounced between the machines on every other movement: the Mac's cursor vanished, and the PC's sat at the edge where it entered, not moving, until you clicked. The Mac now measures movement from where the cursor actually is.

## Updating
Only the Mac needs this version: it still connects to 0.3.0-alpha.14.

On the Mac, quit Legato, install the new version, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Then open Legato and allow the prompts.
