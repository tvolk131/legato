A fix for typing into the Mac's display on Windows.

## Fixed
- **Keys and clicks on the Mac's display go to the Mac again after it has taken control back.** When the Mac's own trackpad or keyboard was used, even a light touch, the Mac took its cursor back. After that, clicks and typing over its picture on Windows went to Windows until the Windows pointer moved. That's why clicking into the picture seemed to focus the Windows window without letting you type on the Mac. Now a click or key over the picture always goes to the Mac. The same applies when the viewer opens under a pointer that hasn't moved yet.

## Updating
Only the Windows PC needs this version: nothing changed on the Mac, and it still connects to 0.3.0-alpha.9.
