The Mac's trackpad can take the pointer from its extra display onto the PC's other monitors, and back.

## New
- **The extra display has edges.** With the Mac's extra display shown on the PC, push the pointer off its side with the Mac's trackpad. It carries on onto the PC's monitor beside the picture, at the same height, and the trackpad then drives the PC. Move back onto the picture and the pointer is on the Mac's extra display again. With the viewer in a window, its edges lead onto the monitor around the window.

## Fixed
- **One pointer, whichever way it got where it is.** After the pointer comes back to the PC from the Mac (across the edge, or off the extra display), the Mac's trackpad now carries on from where the PC's mouse took it. Before, it picked up from where the pointer had left the Mac, which could make the PC's cursor jump back to the extra display. The same goes the other way round.
- **A late message can no longer move the pointer back** just after the other machine takes over.

## Updating
**Both machines need this version.** It won't connect to 0.3.0-alpha.17 or earlier.

On the Mac, quit Legato, install the new version, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Then open Legato and allow the prompts.
