Better logs for connection trouble.

## Changed
- `legato-app.log` now records every connection and disconnection, with how the machines are connected (directly or through a relay), the round-trip time, and why a connection ended.
- It also records when the machine becomes reachable from other networks through a relay, and warns if that relay connection is lost.

The log is in the folder `legato doctor` shows as "State". On Windows that's under `%APPDATA%\tvolk131\Legato\data`, and on the Mac under `~/Library/Application Support/io.github.tvolk131.Legato`.

## Compatibility
Works with 0.3.0-alpha.1 and alpha.2 on the other machine.
