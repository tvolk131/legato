Smoother, faster Mac display on Windows, and stats that show where stutter comes from.

## New
- **Stream quality.** In **Options…** next to your Mac, choose how sharp the picture is sent:
  - **Sharpest:** full resolution.
  - **Balanced:** at most 2560×1440, about twice as quick at 4K.
  - **Fastest:** at most 1920×1080.

  The Mac's display keeps its size and layout; only the picture sent to Windows is smaller, then scaled back up on the PC's GPU. Text is a little softer. Frame-rate choices now follow the size it's sent at, so a 4K display sent Balanced can run at 120 fps.
- **Worst-case stats.** The overlay (F10) adds a third line: the slowest frame on the Mac, in decoding and in display, the longest gap between pictures, and how many pictures the Mac skipped or dropped. When it stutters, this shows whether it's the network, the Mac or the PC.

## Faster
- **4K keeps up better.** Encoding a 4K frame takes most of a 60 fps frame's time, so the Mac fell behind and dropped frames whenever anything else got busy. When frames take most of their time, the Mac now starts the next before the last is out, which gives it about twice the headroom for about one frame more delay. The last frame is still sent as soon as the screen goes still.

## Updating
**Update both machines:** this version won't connect to earlier ones.

On the Mac, quit Legato, install the new version, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Then open Legato and allow the prompts.
