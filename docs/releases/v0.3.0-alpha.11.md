Fixes the Mac and the PC fighting over control while the Mac's display is shown on the PC, and adds logging to track down any typing problem that's left.

## Fixed
- **The Mac no longer grabs control of the PC from its extra display.** While the Mac's cursor was on its extra display, the smallest trackpad movement, even a resting palm, counted as pushing past the MacBook's top edge towards the PC. The Mac then took over the PC and the two traded control every frame. Keys and clicks went to the wrong machine until you moved off the picture and back. The Mac's log showed it as "Controlling …" many times a second.

## Diagnostics
For typing that still goes to the wrong machine, both machines now log why, in `legato-app.log`:
- **On the PC:** when the pointer moves onto the Mac's picture, when it leaves, and when another window covers the picture (naming it). If a key stays on the PC while the Mac's display has focus, it logs the reason.
- **On the Mac:** which app the first keystroke after each entry went to, and when keys arrive while the PC isn't driving the Mac.

## Updating
Update both machines: the fix is on the Mac and the logging is on both. It still connects to 0.3.0-alpha.9 and alpha.10.

On the Mac, quit Legato, install the new version, then run:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Legato.app
tccutil reset Accessibility io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ListenEvent io.github.tvolk131.legato
tccutil reset ScreenCapture io.github.tvolk131.legato
```
Then open Legato and allow the prompts.
